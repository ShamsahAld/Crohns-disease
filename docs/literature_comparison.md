# Comparison with the source preprint and published single-cell studies (plan step 11)

All numbers for 'this analysis' come from the saved tables (33 primary biopsies, 17 inflamed vs 16 non-inflamed, biopsy as the unit, site-adjusted, BH within the analysis, exploratory). Published findings are taken from the cited sources; where a source text was not read in full this is stated.

## 1. Source preprint (Zhou et al., bioRxiv 10.1101/2025.03.26.645552; text of the version posted 31 March 2025, read in full)
The preprint compares non-inflamed, adjacent-to-inflamed and inflamed CD biopsies with non-IBD controls and reports its immune composition tests as Wilcoxon tests with P < 0.05 (the unit of analysis and multiple-testing handling are not stated in the text we read; 'Table S1' was not available to us). It analyses terminal ileum first and then compares with ascending colon.

| Topic | Preprint | This analysis | Assessment |
|---|---|---|---|
| IgG plasma cells | Increased in inflamed tissue in both terminal ileum and ascending colon | IgG share of plasma heavy-chain signal 12.8 % vs 2.1 % (q = 0.018); cell-level calls point the same way (q = 0.061) | **Agrees.** Our plasma-cell *lineage* share does not change (35 vs 30 % of immune cells, q = 0.45). |
| Gamma-delta T cells | Decreased in inflamed tissue (both regions) | Gamma-delta/innate-like IEL lower (1.6 vs 4.7 % of immune cells, q = 0.009; same direction in both sites) | **Agrees.** MrVI view weaker (q = 0.089). |
| TNF-high M1-like macrophages | Increased, order control < non-inflamed < adjacent < inflamed (terminal ileum); correlated with neutrophils | Inflammatory macrophage/monocyte state higher (2.4 vs 0.14 %), but q = 0.15; 88 % of its cells are in three biopsies | **Same direction, weaker evidence.** Our biopsy-level, site-adjusted, BH-controlled test is more conservative; a few biopsies carry the signal. |
| Neutrophils | Enriched in inflamed tissue | Higher (2.0 vs 0.6 %), q = 0.11; 86 % of cells in three biopsies | **Same direction, suggestive only.** |
| Conventional DCs | cDC1 and cDC2 increased in inflamed ileum | Mature DC (LAMP3+) higher (q = 0.062); cDC2 and cDC1 not different; pDC higher (q = 0.004) | **Partly agrees** (activated DCs, not total cDCs). pDC is not discussed in the text we read. |
| CD8 effector T cells / CD3+ T | CD3+ T cells, particularly CD8 effector T cells, increased in inflamed tissue | T/NK share of immune cells *lower* (34 vs 51 %, q = 0.003); CD8 tissue-resident memory lower (q = 0.009); CD8 GZMK+ effector-memory unchanged as share of immune cells (log2 -0.00) and slightly higher within T/NK (+0.49, q = 0.091) | **Not comparable / partly opposite.** Denominators differ (our shares are of immune cells; with all cells as denominator the T/NK share is unchanged, 21.2 to 21.4 %), and the preprint's CD8 labels are annotation-specific. |
| Regulatory T cells | Increased in inflamed ascending colon, unchanged in ileum | Treg-like CD4 higher within T/NK (+1.1 log2, q = 0.037; ileum +0.9, colon +0.3 as share of immune cells, not significant per site) | **Direction agrees; the site pattern is not testable here** (n per site is 14-19). FOXP3 is only weakly expressed in this state. |
| Mast cells | Not discussed in the text we read | Higher in inflamed (all three mast states; q = 0.007-0.028; lineage share 4.8 vs 2.0 %) | **New observation relative to this preprint.** |
| Shared immune DEGs | Genes shared across immune cell types include GBP5, STAT1, IFNG, XBP1, NFKBIA | GBP5 up in T/NK (called and robust); IFNG not significant (FDR 0.27); STAT1/NFKBIA/XBP1 not called (XBP1 is plasma-dominant, guarded) | **Partly agrees** (interferon-induced signature; effector cytokines not reproduced). |
| FAE / epithelial-stromal programmes, BPIFB1, responder analysis | Central message of the preprint | Not addressed (immune cells only) | **Out of scope here.** |

## 2. Other published studies (statements from the sources found by search; full texts not all read)
- **Martin et al., Cell 2019:** a GIMATS module in inflamed ileum consisting of IgG plasma cells, inflammatory mononuclear phagocytes, activated T cells and stromal cells, with inflammatory macrophages and mature DCs central, associated with anti-TNF resistance. *This analysis:* IgG plasma signal higher (supported); inflammatory macrophage/monocyte and mature DC higher in direction but not significant after correction; activated/proliferating T cells higher (G2M and E2F programmes in T/NK; Treg-like CD4 higher within T/NK). Stromal cells and anti-TNF resistance were not tested (immune-only analysis; 24 of 33 biopsies on biologics).
- **Paediatric Crohn's disease before and after anti-TNF (eLife reviewed preprint):** the broad terminal-ileum composition changed little except for increased pDCs and HLA-DR+ mononuclear phagocytes. *This analysis:* pDC higher (q = 0.004) is consistent.
- **IgG plasma cells in IBD (reviews and biopsy studies):** IgG-producing plasma cells and the IgG/IgA ratio rise with mucosal inflammation in ulcerative colitis; IgG production correlates with local inflammation in both Crohn's disease and ulcerative colitis. *This analysis:* consistent.
- **Mast cells in Crohn's disease:** mast cells are increased in diseased small intestine in stricturing Crohn's disease and are a source of IL13 (single-cell study of resections). *This analysis:* mast cells higher in inflamed biopsies, consistent in direction; our biopsies are not strictured resections and the 'activated, IEG-high' state may partly reflect dissociation stress.
- **Large terminal-ileum atlases (IBDverse, medRxiv 2023):** monocytes and an intermediate macrophage population strongly expanded in biopsies from CD patients (reported fold changes in the discovery cohort, 83x and 37x). *This analysis:* classical monocytes and inflammatory macrophage/monocytes higher in direction (q = 0.15), not significant.
- **Myeloid heterogeneity (Nature Communications 2023):** the largest between-patient differences in inflamed intestine are in the myeloid compartment. *This analysis:* consistent with the observation that neutrophil and macrophage signals sit in a few biopsies.
- **Pro-inflammatory NK-like T cells expanded in inflamed intestine (medRxiv 2025, 125-patient cohort):** *This analysis:* NK cells higher within T/NK (q = 0.030) is consistent in direction.

## 3. Pathways
TNF-alpha/NF-kB, interferon-gamma and -alpha, inflammatory response, IL6-JAK-STAT3 and complement programmes in myeloid cells, cDC2 and B cells, and proliferation in T/NK cells, are the expected programmes of active mucosal inflammation and agree with the preprint's inflammatory gene lists (for example GBP5, STAT1, NFKBIA). They are generic inflammatory signals; none is specific to Crohn's disease, and none addresses anti-TNF response.

## 4. What is new or different here, and what should not be claimed
- Supported by an independent analysis and the literature: IgG plasma-cell shift, gamma-delta T loss, pDC increase, inflammatory/interferon/TNF programmes, proliferating T cells.
- Different from the preprint: T/NK share falls as a fraction of immune cells (denominator effect), mast cells are higher, CD8 Trm falls only relative to other immune cells.
- Do not claim: that M1-like macrophages or neutrophils are robustly increased in our data; any treatment-response conclusion; that mast activation is independent of dissociation stress.
